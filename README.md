### Hi!

I'm a PhD student in theoretical evolutionary biology at the Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA), with a background in mathematics. My research combines mathematical and computational approaches to studying evolution.
My two main projects are:

- **[Info-Evo: GP Maps](https://github.com/HilaLifchitz/Info-Evo-GP-Maps)** — A theoretical framework combining population genetics, information theory, and ideas from statistical mechanics to investigate how the structure of fitness landscapes influences evolutionary dynamics, robustness, and evolvability, and ultimately how genotype–phenotype maps themselves may evolve.

- **[TwisstNTern 2](https://github.com/HilaLifchitz/twisstntern_v2)** — A Python package for analyzing and visualizing phylogenetic topology weights in a ternary framework, providing tools to investigate genealogical discordance, identify patterns of asymmetry, and explore the evolutionary processes underlying genomic variation.
